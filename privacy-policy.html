Overview

This privacy policy explains how MK Damp & Timber LTD collects, uses and stores information from visitors to our website and customers of our services.

Data collected

We collect information that you provide directly, such as name, contact details, property information and survey requirements.

When you contact us by phone, email or via the website, we may retain details of your enquiry and any supporting images or documents.

How we use your data

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, arrange surveys, deliver services and manage our relationship with you.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share information with trusted suppliers who support project delivery when necessary.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve performance, remember your preferences and support key sections such as contact forms.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. We do not use cookies for advertising purposes on this site.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, request corrections, object to processing and ask for data deletion where applicable.

If you would like to exercise your rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.
