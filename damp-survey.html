Service overview

Our damp surveys cover rising damp, penetrating damp and condensation. We inspect floors, walls, roofs, lofts, and timber elements. Each survey includes a full written report, photographs, and a recommended scope of works for permanent repair.

What we check

  • Rising damp and damp proof course failures
  • Penetrating damp from leaks and poor external detailing
  • Condensation and ventilation issues
  • Wet rot and dry rot in structural timbers
  • Roofing defects, guttering and valley problems
