About Us

MK Damp and Timber LTD

are experts in all aspects of residential and commercial damp proofing and wet rot and dry rot remediation. We have over 25 years experience in identifying problems, the source of the damp problems and providing permanent solutions. We regularly sub contract for some of the largest damp proofing and building companies in the UK and have built up an enviable reputation with many satisfied customers from Milton Keynes to North London.

We do more than damp proofing though; Contact us today to discuss a full conversion of your cellar or loft, once we have permanently taken care of the damp issues. Let us take care of your pitched and flat roofing needs, extension builds either big or small or any other general building work.
